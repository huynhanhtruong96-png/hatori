ABOUT US

  • HATORI Import Export Co., Ltd. is one of the leading exporters and suppliers of Asian Food & Beverage products in Vietnam. We are happy to supply well-known brands and high-quality products to wholesalers and retailers in over 10 countries worldwide. Our strength lies in excellent customer service, quality products, and competitive pricing. With extensive experience in Asian products, our dedicated sales team focuses on understanding customer needs and providing responsive, customized solutions to supermarkets, wholesalers, and distributors across Asia, Europe, Oceania, and island markets.
